# Design and Development of Electric-Go-Kart-with-Solar-Charging
This project focuses on the design and development of an electric go-kart powered by a rechargeable battery system with an integrated solar photovoltaic charging unit.

The system combines electric propulsion with renewable energy to provide an eco-friendly mobility solution. The project involved vehicle design, electrical system integration, battery and solar charging integration, and evaluation of the overall vehicle performance.

## Key Features

* Electric-powered go-kart
* Rechargeable battery-based propulsion system
* Solar photovoltaic charging integration
* Mechanical and electrical system integration
* Eco-friendly and sustainable mobility solution

↓

## Technologies & Areas

* Electric Vehicle Systems
* Solar Photovoltaic Systems

* Vehicle System Integration

## Outcome

The project demonstrates the integration of electric vehicle technology with renewable solar energy to reduce dependence on conventional grid charging and provide a sustainable transportation solution.
